# Delivery SOP (MVP — pickup + manual delivery, SPEC §13)

- Every order has `fulfilment_method`: Customer Pickup | Delivery.
- Delivery requires: address, contact person/phone, preferred datetime, notes, charge.
- Status: Not Required | Pending Arrangement | Arranged | Out for Delivery | Delivered.
- Staff update status manually. No fleet app, no GPS in MVP.
- Delivery charge = `Delivery & Setup` service Item, posted to owning company.
